How immunity spreads

  1. 01

    Recognize

    A scout on your computer watches what processes do, not what they are called. When a chain of actions starts to look like ransomware, it's frozen mid-step.

  2. 02

    Contain

    A soldier wakes inside a sealed sandbox and tries containment moves until one stops the threat without breaking the apps you rely on.

  3. 03

    Prove

    Five nodes test the cure. Three have to sign before it's written to the chain, so a single bad actor can't push a fake one.

  4. 04

    Inherit

    Every connected device pulls the cure and is protected before the threat ever reaches it. If a cure misbehaves, it can be switched off everywhere at once.
